## PhD opportunities

## ESRC South-West Doctoral Training Programme

We (the University of Bristol) are part of the [ESRC South-West Doctoral Training Partnership](https://www.swdtp.ac.uk/prospective-students-and-fellows/esrc-funded-studentships/). This is basically a pot of money from the Economic and Social Sciences Research Council that goes to a conglomerate of several (currently eight) universities in the region. I am listed as a potential supervisor in the subject area of 'Psychology' and on the pathway of 'Advanced quantitative methods'. 

Given the number of partners involved (several Universities, the schools within those universities that fall within ESRC remit, all the academics in those schools), the reality is that only stellar candidates will be competitive for this award. If you think you are a stellar candidate and you share some of my research interests that can be branded as 'social science' (e.g. work on decision-making), then get in touch and we can explore possible research projects. However, if my judgement is that you are unlikely to be competitive given your academic history, I will flag this at an early stage so that neither of us waste our time. 

The application deadline for this scheme is **12 January 2027**. See also the job advert on [jobs.ac.uk](https://www.jobs.ac.uk/job/DTB766/economic-and-social-science-research-council-phd-studentships)
