## PhD opportunities

### ESRC South-West Doctoral Training Programme

We (the University of Bristol) are part of the [ESRC South-West Doctoral Training Partnership](https://www.swdtp.ac.uk/prospective-students-and-fellows/esrc-funded-studentships/). This is basically a pot of money from the Economic and Social Sciences Research Council that goes to a conglomerate of several (currently eight) universities in the region. I am listed as a potential supervisor in the subject area of 'Psychology' and on the pathway of 'Advanced quantitative methods'. 

Given the number of partners involved (several Universities, the schools within those universities that fall within ESRC remit, all the academics in those schools), the reality is that only stellar candidates will be competitive for this award. It is hard to be clear-cut about what "stellar" means, but you probably need to have an undergraduate and Masters degree from good, research-intensive universities and achieved top scores (e.g. first-class in the UK, distinction at Masters level, cum laude elsewhere). I am happy to advise if you are interested and I will be honest so that you do not waste time applying if you are unlikely to be competitive. 

If you did want to apply under my supervision, we would need to come up with a strong project proposal. Given the nature of the funding body, the project would need to have a social science flavour. So hardcore psychophysics will not be funded through this route; but something like belief updating based on visualised evidence might be. If you are interested in the part of my work that could be branded as 'social science', do get in touch and I am happy to explore possible projects with you. 

The application deadline for this scheme is **12 January 2027**. See also the job advert on [jobs.ac.uk](https://www.jobs.ac.uk/job/DTB766/economic-and-social-science-research-council-phd-studentships)
